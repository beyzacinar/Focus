Focus – Screen Time Reflection Assistant

Focus is a digital well-being application designed to help users understand their smartphone usage habits, reflect on their screen time, and develop healthier digital routines.
Rather than relying on strict app restrictions, screen locks, or stressful streak-based challenges, Focus promotes a calm, mindful, and sustainable approach to digital well-being.
The project aims to combine screen-time insights, behavioral pattern analysis, and personalized recommendations to help users recognize excessive usage, make more intentional decisions, and find a healthier balance between their digital and offline lives.

Key Objectives

Screen Time Awareness: Help users understand when and how they use their smartphones.
Behavioral Reflection: Identify usage patterns and encourage mindful digital habits.
Personalized Insights: Provide meaningful feedback to support healthier routines.
Positive Reinforcement: Reward intentional moments away from screens without creating pressure or guilt.
Digital Well-Being: Encourage long-term behavioral improvements rather than temporary restrictions.

Our Philosophy
Less pressure. More awareness. Better habits.
Focus believes that healthier screen habits begin with understanding, not restriction. Our goal is to make digital well-being feel supportive, achievable, and rewarding.
